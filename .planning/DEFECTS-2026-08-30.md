# mdlint defects — external-oracle verification, 2026-08-30

Source: a four-lens adversarial verification of the Run 2 product (rule
correctness, CLI contract, robustness, shipped-vs-claimed). Every entry below
was produced by running the shipped CLI against crafted cases and against
27,013 lines of real Markdown, and comparing with an INDEPENDENT
implementation — not with mdlint's own tests, which pass 173/173 at 99.83%
coverage while these defects are present.

**Two tiers.** Tier A was confirmed by two independent refuters that each
reproduced the command and checked the expectation against the spec. Tier B
was reproduced once by a tester; its refuters were interrupted by a model
rate limit, so treat the expectation as proposed, not settled — confirm it
before fixing.

Rule of work: fix the cause, not the symptom, and never regress a family that
currently matches the oracle (MDW01-04, MDS01-03, MDT03 are exact on the
corpus; keep them exact).
